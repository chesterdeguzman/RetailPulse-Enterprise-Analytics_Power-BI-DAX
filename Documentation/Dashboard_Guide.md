# Dashboard Guide

## 1. Executive Overview
KPI cards: Total Revenue, Gross Profit, Gross Margin %, Orders, Active Customers, Revenue Attainment %. Trend chart for monthly revenue and budget; territory bar chart; category mix; channel mix.

## 2. Sales Performance
Revenue, profit, discount and unit trends; category and product drill-down; channel and ship-mode analysis; conditional-format performance matrix.

## 3. Customer Analytics
New and repeat customers, customer value tiers, revenue per customer, segment mix and retention indicators.

## 4. Product & Returns
Top/bottom products, margin distribution, return rate, refund impact and return reasons.

## 5. Regional Performance
Territory/region/city analysis, budget attainment, employee contribution and geographic map.

## 6. KPI Explorer
Field-parameter-style KPI selection powered by the KPI Selector table and dynamic DAX measures.
